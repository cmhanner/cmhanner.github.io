Gladys West: Inventor of The GPS

Dr. Gladys West is a renowed mathmetician and programmer responsible for the creation of the GPS. Combining complex mathmatics and efficient programming she was able to process satellite data to generate accurate models of Earth. That work laid the groundwork for the GPS. Originally a maths and science teacher from Virginia, she was hired in 1956 to work at Naval Proving Ground being the second black woman ever hired and one of only four black employees.
