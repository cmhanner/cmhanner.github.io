Gladys West: Inventor of The GPS

Born: October 27, 1930, age: 94!

Dr. Gladys West is a renowed mathmetician and programmer responsible for the creation of the GPS. Combining complex mathmatics and efficient programming she was able to process satellite data to generate accurate models of Earth. That work laid the groundwork for the GPS. Originally a maths and science teacher from Virginia, she was hired in 1956 to work at Naval Proving Ground being the second black woman ever hired and one of only four black employees.

She was project manager of one of the first satellites that could sense oceans remotely. 1970's - 1980's she designed devloped and tested and used programs to constantly create better and better models of the Earth. She had to use complex algorithms to consider the constanly changing gravitational, tidal, and random forces that change Earth's shape. This data ultimately led to the Global Positioning System (GPS) which we all use.

Education

  1. B.S. in Mathematics in 1955
  2. M.S. in Mathematics in 1973
  3. PHD in Public Administration and Policy Affairs in 2000 at age 70
